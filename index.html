The rule, in one line. You may spend at most 90 days inside the Schengen Area in any rolling 180-day window. Eurodays 90 tracks that window for you — every day, on both platforms.

Everything you need to stay compliant

Built for frequent travellers, digital nomads, and anyone whose visa-free time is counted — not guessed.

The 90/180-day calculator

A live count of your remaining allowance, recomputed from the rolling 180-day window that border authorities actually apply — not a simple counter. Days remaining, days used, and your compliance zone at a glance.

Core feature

"When can I return?" planner

Enter a trip length and a not-before date. Eurodays 90 names the earliest legal entry date and your exit-by date — and verifies every single day in the range, warning you of the first date a stay would breach 90.

Full stay log

Every trip grouped by year with past / current / future status, search, and filters — with a compliance summary for the whole year and exact counted days for each stay.

Custom-date check

Pick any date — past or future — and see how many days were used, how many were left, and your compliance status for that day's 180-day window, with a per-stay breakdown.

Built on exact math

All tallies use integer UTC day arithmetic — immune to time zones, daylight-saving shifts, and month-length quirks. Both your entry and exit day always count, exactly as the rule requires.

Private by design

No account, no sync, no network calls. Your stays live on your device alone and never leave it — export them as CSV anytime for backup or your own records.

Travel with certainty, not guesswork

Stop recalculating windows by hand at 11pm before a flight. Eurodays 90 keeps your allowance current, every single day.
