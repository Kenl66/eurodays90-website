Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Eurodays 90 ("the app") is designed to be private by default. This policy explains — in plain terms — what data the app handles and what it does not.

1. What we store

All information you enter into the app — your stay dates, notes, and app preferences — is stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party by the app itself. We operate no servers that receive your data.

2. What we do not collect

  • No account or sign-up is required, and none is offered.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No location data, passport data, or identity documents.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, or microphone.

3. Purchases

The optional one-time lifetime unlock is processed through your device's app store (Apple App Store or Google Play). Purchase validation is handled by RevenueCat, which receives a non-identifying purchase token to confirm entitlement. RevenueCat's own privacy policy governs that processing: revenuecat.com/privacy. We never see your name, email, or payment details.

4. Backups and export

You may export your stays as a CSV file. That export is created on your device and stays wherever you choose to save or share it. If your operating system's automatic device backup is enabled, your app data may be included in that backup under Apple's or Google's privacy terms.

5. Deleting your data

Clearing all stays in the app (Info → Your data) or uninstalling the app permanently removes all locally stored data. No copy exists elsewhere.

6. Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect personal information at all.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will be highlighted in the app's release notes.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at support@eurodays90.app.
